01 / What I do

Front to back, and everything underneath

I build web software end to end. I write React and TypeScript on the front, Node.js services and REST APIs behind them, and I own the AWS, CI/CD and DNS underneath. In practice I am usually the person who can answer for the whole path rather than one layer of it.

Most of my work now is AI systems, and most of that is retrieval quality, guardrails and failure paths. The interesting engineering is rarely the model itself. It is everything around it.

I like problems that cross layers, where the bug could be in a React component or in a webhook payload three services away and you genuinely do not know which yet. The parts nobody demos usually end up mine: deploy pipelines, DNS, the job that runs at 3am. They are the difference between something that works in a demo and something that works on a Tuesday.

Before this: three years freelancing for my own clients, two years as a senior service technician, training the people who came after me, and an Associate of Science in Computer Science from Langara.

Away from the keyboard: FPS games, more music than is reasonable, and an ongoing pomegranate juice habit.
