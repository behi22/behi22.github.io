About

Most of my week goes into AI agents and voice: prompt work, RAG pipelines, and the guardrails that stop an agent wandering off script halfway through a call. The rest goes into the unglamorous parts that make it survive contact with real users, like the deploy pipeline my team ships through.

I like problems that cross the whole stack, where the bug could be in a React component or in a Twilio webhook and you genuinely do not know which yet.

Away from the keyboard: FPS games, more music than is reasonable, and an ongoing pomegranate juice habit.
