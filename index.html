01 / What I do

Whole stack, ugly middle included

I write React and TypeScript on the front, Node.js services and REST APIs behind them, and I own the AWS, CI/CD and DNS underneath. The parts of a product that nobody demos are usually the parts I end up responsible for.

A lot of my current work is AI: agent systems on the OpenAI and Claude APIs, prompt engineering, RAG pipelines, knowledge bases, and the guardrails that stop an agent wandering off script. Voice is one slice of that, not the whole job. The other slices are webhook pipelines, lead ingestion, CRM and scheduling automation, and internal tools that replace work people were doing by hand.

I like problems that cross layers, where the bug could be in a React component or in a webhook payload and you genuinely do not know which yet. I review PRs, onboard engineers, sit in on technical interviews, and argue about product direction.

Before this: three years freelancing for my own clients, two years fixing other people's computers at a retail bench, and an Associate of Science in Computer Science from Langara.

Away from the keyboard: FPS games, more music than is reasonable, and an ongoing pomegranate juice habit.
