01 / What I do

Agents that answer the phone

Most of my week goes into AI agents and voice: prompt work, RAG pipelines, and the guardrails that stop an agent wandering off script halfway through a call. There are 25+ agents live in production handling thousands of calls a week.

The rest goes into the parts nobody demos. I own the architecture across our AI platforms and integrations, built the webhook and lead-ingestion pipelines from scratch, and maintain the CI/CD my team of five ships through.

I like problems that cross the whole stack, where the bug could be in a React component or in a Twilio webhook and you genuinely do not know which yet. Before this I freelanced for three years and studied computer science at Langara.

Away from the keyboard: FPS games, more music than is reasonable, and an ongoing pomegranate juice habit.
